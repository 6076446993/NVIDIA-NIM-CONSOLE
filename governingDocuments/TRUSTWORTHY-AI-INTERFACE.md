# Trustworthy AI Interface Responsibilities

This repository implements the Nexus Trustworthy AI Governance Contract only within NVIDIA-NIM-CONSOLE's existing interface and routing authority.

## Required controls
- Present the producing AI/council identity and provenance when available and relevant.
- Present authoritative verification/governance/custody status without upgrading weaker states.
- Surface material limitations, uncertainty, dissent, failed verification, STOP, rejection, or supersession when relevant to a proposed action.
- Preserve human control over proposed actions where Nexus authority requires authorization.
- Do not treat model consensus, interface presentation, or successful generation as Crucible verification.
- Preserve repository/task/version references needed to challenge or reproduce a material result where feasible.
- Fail closed rather than claiming completion when required verification, authority, or provenance is missing.
- Do not expose secrets, protected evidence, or private source in the name of transparency.

This document does not grant the console verification, governance, custody, or repository-promotion authority.
